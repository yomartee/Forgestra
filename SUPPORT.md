# Support scope

Experimental local CLI/library and optional read-only stdio only; see the
[capability matrix](docs/CAPABILITIES.md). No production SLA, hosted factory service,
customer repair executor or functioning live generator integration is claimed.

Use the private routes in [security](SECURITY.md) and [conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
for those reports. Each has a seven-calendar-day acknowledgment target, not a
guaranteed fix/resolution deadline. No general support channel, response commitment
for ordinary support requests, backup responder or independent conduct escalation
is designated. Do not disclose private data in public.
